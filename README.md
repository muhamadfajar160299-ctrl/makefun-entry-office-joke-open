A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-aldy998-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-the-office-joke-open-9920f232) entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: the Office Joke Open** — one line of office comedy, at most 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The joke

> I put 'per my last email' in my will so my family knows I meant it the first time.

## How it runs

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.txt` byte-identical to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (mode 0644), with no temp file:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```
